OpenAI vs Google DeepMind vs Anthropic: how they compare

By the Great AI Race desk · Nov 17, 2026 · 6 min read

OpenAI, Google DeepMind and Anthropic are the three labs people compare most often. All three build frontier models, sell them to consumers and businesses, and spend heavily on compute. But they are built very differently: one is a fast-growing private company with a huge consumer app, one is a research lab inside one of the world's largest companies, and one is a safety-focused lab preparing for a public listing. Here is how they compare on the numbers, as of Nov 17, 2026.

About these numbers. Every figure below comes from the public source linked next to it and carries the date that source covers. Where a lab does not publish a number, we say so rather than estimate it. Scores come from the Great AI Race methodology as of Nov 17, 2026.

Money: three very different balance sheets

Comparing money across these three is tricky, because they are not the same kind of company. Google DeepMind is part of Alphabet, so its Money lane uses Alphabet's market cap of about $3.64 trillion and Alphabet's total revenue. Alphabet does not report AI separately, so this flatters Google relative to an AI-only figure. We flag the same issue for Meta, Alibaba and xAI.

OpenAI is private. Bloomberg reported a new round that values it at $1.25 trillion, with about $11 billion wired so far. OpenAI had not confirmed it at the time of the report. The Financial Times reported about $50 billion of annualized revenue at the end of the September quarter, up 77% from a year earlier.

Anthropic's last private round, its Series H, valued it at $965 billion. Its updated IPO prospectus reports a run rate of roughly $81 billion as of Oct 31, 2026. Its IPO terms imply a valuation between about $1.2 trillion and $1.4 trillion, depending on the share count used. A run rate is not the same as trailing revenue: Anthropic's revenue for the 12 months to Sep 30 was $35.3 billion.

Reach: ChatGPT is still the default

ChatGPT remains the most visited AI destination on the web, with 55.7% of generative AI website traffic in October. Gemini had 24.2% and Claude 11.5%. Claude gained 1.9 points in a month, the largest gain of the three. Google reports more than 1 billion Gemini monthly active users, and reporting based on internal figures puts ChatGPT at about 1 billion weekly users. Anthropic does not publish Claude user counts. Under our rules that means its Reach lane uses web share alone.

Brains: a close fight at the top

Benchmarks move fast. After Claude Fable 5.2 launched on Nov 9, it scored 65 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at max effort. Gemini 4 Carbon scored 60 and GPT-6 Astra 57, per OfficeChai's summary. Google says Carbon ranks first on several agent and coding benchmarks, including CursorBench 4.0, in its general-availability post. No single benchmark settles the question, which is why the Brains lane also uses Text Arena rank when one is published.

Availability matters too. Anthropic's Mythos 5.2 is limited to vetted organizations, and OpenAI's GPT-6 Cyber, launched Nov 5, is limited to approved security customers. Our Brains lane only counts scores reported for each lab's top model.

Muscle and momentum

On Epoch AI's list of the largest AI data centers by current power, Anthropic's New Carlisle site with Amazon is first at about 1,037 MW, and OpenAI's Stargate Abilene is listed at about 824 MW. Google's biggest single site is smaller, but Google runs a large, spread-out TPU fleet that a single-site measure misses.

All three ship often. In the six months to mid-November, Anthropic shipped seven major releases by our count, OpenAI six and Google at least five, including Gemini 4 Hydrogen on Nov 12.

Openness

Google publishes the Gemma family of open models, most recently Gemma 4 in April 2026. OpenAI released gpt-oss open weights in 2025. Anthropic has not released open-weight models. Read more in open-weight vs closed AI models explained.

So which is "best"?

It depends on what you weight. If reach matters most, OpenAI leads. If financial scale matters most, Google leads. If you care most about top benchmark scores, compute and release pace, Anthropic leads. On the track, set Reach to 0 and Brains to 5 and see how the order shifts. Each of these companies is doing serious work. The scoreboard is just a way to see the race from more than one angle.

FAQ

Is OpenAI bigger than Anthropic?

By reported valuation, yes for now: a Bloomberg-reported round values OpenAI at $1.25 trillion, while Anthropic's last private round was $965 billion. By reported run-rate revenue, Anthropic (about $81 billion as of Oct 31, 2026) is ahead of OpenAI (about $50 billion at the end of September), per the sources on each fact card.

Which has more users, ChatGPT or Gemini?

ChatGPT had 55.7% of generative AI website traffic in October 2026 versus 24.2% for Gemini, per Similarweb data reported by OfficeChai. Google reports more than 1 billion Gemini monthly active users. The two companies measure users differently.

Does Anthropic release open-weight models?

No Anthropic open-weight model has been found as of Nov 17, 2026, so Anthropic scores 0 in the Openness lane.

Why is Google DeepMind's valuation so high?

Google DeepMind is part of Alphabet, so the race uses Alphabet's market cap, about $3.64 trillion on Nov 13, 2026. Alphabet does not report AI separately.
