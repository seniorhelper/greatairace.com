The biggest AI labs by valuation in November 2026

By the Great AI Race desk · Nov 17, 2026 · 5 min read

AI valuations are some of the most quoted numbers in tech, and some of the most confusing. Some labs are public companies with a live share price. Some are private and valued by their last funding round. Some are divisions inside giant companies whose market cap covers far more than AI. Below are the latest figures we could verify for the nine labs on the Great AI Race track, as of Nov 17, 2026.

About these numbers. Every figure below comes from the public source linked next to it and carries the date that source covers. Where a lab does not publish a number, we say so rather than estimate it. Scores come from the Great AI Race methodology as of Nov 17, 2026.

Read the fine print: three kinds of number

1. Parent-company market caps

Google DeepMind, Meta's AI group, the Qwen team and xAI all sit inside larger public companies. Their figures are the parent's full market cap: Alphabet, Meta, Alibaba and SpaceX. None of these companies breaks out a separate value for its AI unit, so these numbers include search ads, social apps, e-commerce and rockets. In the race, this gives those labs a higher Money score than an AI-only figure would. We flag it on every fact card.

xAI is the clearest case. The $1.87 trillion figure is SpaceX's total market cap at the Nov 16 close. It is not a valuation of xAI on its own.

2. Private funding rounds

OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Moonshot AI and Mistral AI are valued by their funding rounds. A round sets a price for new shares at one moment. It is not a daily market price. OpenAI's latest figure is a round reported by Bloomberg at $1.25 trillion, with about $11 billion raised so far. Its last confirmed primary round was $852 billion in March 2026. We show both on the OpenAI fact card.

3. An IPO in progress

Anthropic is in the middle of an IPO. Its terms call for 825 million shares at $70 to $78 each, with trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT expected around Nov 19. Depending on the share count used, the range implies about $1.2 trillion at the midpoint or about $1.4 trillion fully diluted at the top. We keep the $965 billion Series H figure in the race until the IPO actually prices, then switch to the market cap.

How the numbers moved in 2026

The private labs raised at rising prices this year. OpenAI's confirmed round in March 2026 valued it at $852 billion, and the round reported in November puts it at $1.25 trillion. Anthropic's Series H in May raised $65 billion at $965 billion. DeepSeek raised $7.4 billion at about $50 billion in its first outside round in June. Moonshot AI raised $3.5 billion at $35 billion after launching Kimi K3, and Mistral AI closed a €3 billion Series D at €21 billion post-money in September.

Public market caps move every trading day. Alphabet was valued at about $3.87 trillion on Oct 26, 2026 and about $3.64 trillion at the Nov 13 close, a drop of roughly 6% in under three weeks. That is why every figure in the race carries its own date. A market cap from last month can be off by hundreds of billions of dollars today.

How we pick which figure to use

  • Public parent: the latest market cap we can verify from market data, with the close date.
  • Private lab: the most recent primary funding round. When a new round is reported but not confirmed, we say so in the note and show the last confirmed round next to it.
  • Secondary-market prices and unverified claims are shown as notes, not scored.
  • No number at all: we show "Not disclosed" and never estimate.

All of these figures are in our public data file, race.json, with a source link and date for each one.

Valuation is not the whole race

Money is one of six lanes in the Great AI Race, and it uses both valuation and revenue on a log scale so that a multi-trillion-dollar parent does not flatten everyone else. Revenue tells a different story: Anthropic reports a run rate of about $81 billion, ahead of OpenAI's roughly $50 billion, while Alphabet's total revenue run rate is over $500 billion but not split out for AI. See every lane on the track, or compare the top three in OpenAI vs Google DeepMind vs Anthropic.

Why the smaller labs still matter

DeepSeek, Moonshot AI and Mistral AI are valued at a small fraction of the leaders, yet they publish some of the most capable open-weight models in the world. Moonshot released Kimi K3.1 Pro weights on Nov 5, and Mistral released Mistral Large 4 weights in late October. A high Openness score keeps these labs competitive in the composite. FAQ

What is the most valuable AI company in 2026?

By market cap, Alphabet (Google DeepMind's parent) is the largest at about $3.64 trillion on Nov 13, 2026. Among AI-only private labs, OpenAI's reported $1.25 trillion round is the highest figure.

Is xAI worth $1.87 trillion?

No single figure for xAI alone is published. $1.87 trillion is SpaceX's total market cap on Nov 16, 2026. SpaceX includes xAI along with its rocket and satellite businesses.

What is Anthropic's valuation?

Anthropic's last private round, its Series H, valued it at $965 billion. Its IPO terms imply roughly $1.2 trillion to $1.4 trillion, depending on the share count used. The Great AI Race will switch to the market cap once the IPO prices.

No. They are reported figures shown for information and entertainment only. They change often and are not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
