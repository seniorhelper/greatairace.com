About the Great AI Race

A scoreboard for the biggest race in tech, with the receipts attached.

The Great AI Race turns the AI industry into something you can see at a glance: nine labs on a giant neon track, placed by real numbers. Every figure links to where it came from and the date it covers. When a lab doesn't publish a number, we say "Not disclosed" instead of guessing.

The race is headquartered in Denver, Colorado, and refreshed from public filings, market data, benchmarks and data center research, with a daily update target. The flat track is live now. 3D and VR views are coming.

Who is on the track

Only labs that train and run their own models are racers. Apps built on someone else's model are not. The current field: OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta, Anthropic, xAI, Alibaba Qwen, DeepSeek, Moonshot AI and Mistral AI. Big AI Brain, a home-grown AI built from scratch, runs in its own exhibition lane.

Independent by design

Great AI Race is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, xAI, Meta, DeepSeek, Alibaba, Moonshot AI, Mistral AI or any other lab. Lab names are used only to identify them. The racer emblems are original designs, not company logos.

Not investment advice, not betting

Scores are a fun, transparent way to compare labs. They are not a recommendation to buy or sell anything, and the race is not a betting or gambling product.

Data for people and agents

Everything on the track comes from one public file, race.json. The Data page explains the fields.
