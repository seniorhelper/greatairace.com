Race FAQ

What is the Great AI Race?

An independent scoreboard that ranks nine AI labs as racers on a neon track. Positions come from six lanes of sourced data: Money, Reach, Brains, Muscle, Momentum and Openness.

Who is winning the AI race right now?

With equal lane weights, OpenAI leads at 80.5, followed by Google DeepMind (73.9) and Meta (65.7), as of Nov 17, 2026. Change the weights and the order can change.

How is the composite score calculated?

Each lane is scored 0 to 100 against the nine labs. The composite is the weighted average of the six lanes, with equal weights by default. You can change the weights with the sliders and watch the order change.

Where does the data come from?

Company filings and announcements, Artificial Analysis, Text Arena, Epoch AI, Similarweb, Sensor Tower, QuestMobile, market data and press reports. Every number on a fact card links to its source and shows its date.

Why do some labs show "Not disclosed"?

Some labs do not publish user counts, revenue or data center capacity. We never estimate a missing number. A missing input scores zero in its lane, which is noted on the fact card.

Is this investment advice?

No. The Great AI Race is for information and entertainment only. It is not investment advice and it is not a betting or gambling product.

Is the site affiliated with any AI lab?

No. The Great AI Race is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any lab shown on the track. Lab names are used only to identify them, and the racer emblems are original designs.

What is Big AI Brain doing on the track?

Big AI Brain runs in a separate Exhibition lane, in dead last for now. It is a home-grown AI built from scratch with no outside LLM, and it grows as people feed it. It is not ranked against the labs.

How often is the data updated?

The race runs from one data file, /data/race.json, with a daily update target. Each value carries its own date, and the current data is as of Nov 17, 2026.

Can AI agents use the data?

Yes. The full dataset is public JSON at https://www.greatairace.com/data/race.json, documented on the Data page, with a source URL and date on every figure.
