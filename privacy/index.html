Privacy policy

Last updated Nov 17, 2026.

This policy explains what information the Great AI Race (www.greatairace.com) collects and how it is used. The site is operated from Denver, Colorado, USA.

Information you give us

If you sign up for race alerts or use the contact form, we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address and message. We use them only to send the alerts you asked for or to reply to you. We do not sell your personal information. You can ask us to delete it at any time through the contact form.

Information stored in your browser

The site saves your color skin and race weight settings in your browser's local storage so they are remembered on your next visit. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Advertising and Google AdSense

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Where required by law, such as for visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a consent message may ask for your choice before personalized ads are shown.

Third-party services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers may receive your IP address and basic browser information as part of serving their content. Form submissions are sent to our own lead server for delivery.

Links to other sites

Fact cards and articles link to outside sources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including California, you may have the right to know, correct or delete personal information we hold about you. To make a request, use the contact form.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
